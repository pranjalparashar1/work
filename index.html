  • Best Funds Analysis!This analysis and its presentation got me hired as a portfolio manager in my current role.PDF · 5 pp · Feb 2026
  • Portfolio analysis toolInteractive · anonymised
    The client moment behind it

    We review a client's mutual fund portfolio on several fronts: returns against the benchmark, fees, sectors and market-cap mix.

    One client had ₹76L, half in direct plans and half in regular plans. The usual way to show the fee is as a percentage: about 1.5% a year on regular plans against 0.5% on direct, so roughly 1% extra. Then you move on to the next drag.

    I showed it in rupees instead, fund by fund: what the client had actually paid in regular-plan fees, next to what each fund had earned over its benchmark.

    To get the numbers right, I went through the transaction files and worked out each fund's XIRR using the actual timing of every investment. Applying an average fee % to today's value would have misstated the cost, because money that went in later has paid fees for less time.

    The client's reaction: "I didn't know I was paying this much for funds A, B and C. D is fine, but A, B and C are a surprise."

    Same data, a simpler way to show it. It took knowing what to show, how to say it, and the finance to calculate it properly.
